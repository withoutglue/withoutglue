### Featured project

**[Cyclist Maneuver Anticipation with V-JEPA 2](https://github.com/withoutglue/cycling-anticipation)** (MSc thesis)
Can a frozen video foundation model predict what a cyclist will do at an intersection?
- Pipeline from 17.5 h of self-recorded helmet-camera video and GPS to 6,285 clips, labelled
  automatically with OpenStreetMap
- Four probe architectures on frozen V-JEPA 2 features, trained on CSC's GPU cluster (PyTorch, SLURM)
- 51–56 % macro accuracy (chance 25 %), plus a seen vs. unseen intersection analysis showing
  how much of it comes from location familiarity
